Privacy policy

BMP Autopilot Flight Log · last updated 5 October 2026

The BMP Flight Log is a private progress tracker for participants of one Business Management Program batch. It is run by Azhar Qazi for the batch, not as a commercial service.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. Nothing else from your Google account is read.
  • The progress you enter yourself: action items ticked, notes, weekly habits, books read, call-sign and company name.

How it is used

  • Your email is checked against the list of invited participants. Only invited people can enter.
  • Your progress is saved so you can see it on any device.
  • The leaderboard shows your call-sign, company and scores to other invited participants. Your email is never shown to anyone except the organiser.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Google Sheet owned by the organiser. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing your data

Ask the organiser at azhar.a.qazi@gmail.com and your rows will be deleted.
